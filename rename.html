Google Sheets sync

Paste your deployed Apps Script URL below to enable cross-device sync. Once connected, data saves to both your browser and your Google Sheet automatically.

Setup steps:
1. Open your Google Sheet → Extensions → Apps Script
2. Paste the contents of CFI_AppsScript.gs
3. Click Deploy → New deployment → Web app
4. Set "Execute as: Me" and "Access: Anyone"
5. Copy the deployment URL and paste it above
